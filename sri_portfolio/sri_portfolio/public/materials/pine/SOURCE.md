# Pine textures

Source: https://polyhaven.com/a/pine_tree_01
License: CC0 (https://polyhaven.com/license)
Authors credited by Poly Haven: Rico Cilliers (modeling), Rob Tuytel (photography).

The original full model is not included. `needles.webp` is the first needle sprig cropped from the 1K twig diffuse/alpha atlas, combined into RGBA and resized to 256×512. `bark.webp` is the 1K bark diffuse resized to 512×512. Both encoded as WebP. The portfolio's branch/needle-card geometry is authored separately in `forest-geometry.mjs`.
