# Yosemite-inspired outdoor unit

Reference read: [National Park Service, Yosemite geology](https://www.nps.gov/yose/learn/nature/geology.htm), September 22, 2026. Design cues are the broad floor of a U-shaped valley, contrasting sheer and rounded granite forms, and a hanging waterfall descending into the valley. This is scenery within Sri's invented world, not a mapped replica or evidence of a visit.

Reserve full rock/canopy footprints. Use fewer, purposeful vegetation groups instead of a denser uniform ring. Main adjusts existing light direction and fill; no extra shadow lights. ## Integration and screenshot gate

The initial Cursor implementation did not pass visual acceptance. Its stream was partly buried in the coarse terrain, falling ribbon collapsed along vertical spans, the dome had a seam, meadow indices were invalid, and the lookout top was back-facing. The main integration review in `delegation/yosemite-main-review.md` led to actual fixes and regression coverage. The flat meadow sheet was removed after a screenshot exposed its geometric edges. Terrain now carries subdued organic forest/earth color, with sparse olive grasses instead of pale repeated rows.

Expanded the trail's usable clearing to an offset inner radius 23 (center −2, −5), outer 42; the world radius stays 190. Local terrain refinement around the channel/lake supplies the geometry needed to contain the stream and shares the exact resulting triangles with ground queries. Coarse patch boundaries retain the original edge interpolation. Granite forms now include a wall/lip, joined cut dome and long irregular ridge. The watercourse has a descending curved centerline ending at the flat lake. Motion controls pause its shader clock.

Screenshot reinspection at 1280×800 and 390×844 caught a pine directly across the overlook sight line. The whole canopy now clears both photo cameras, and phone view offsets keep the fall's bottom above the controls. The level stone lookout and ramp use the same elevation as visitor feet. Explore on foot from the overlook starts there facing its approach path. Phone walking visibly crossed onto the connected main path; cruise toggled and stopped, keyboard controls/exit were exercised, and reading stopped motion. Escape returned focus to Read this place. The lake click emitted “A ripple of curiosity.” No new messages were submitted through the contact form.

Final inspected scenes: phone and desktop valley arrivals; phone/day material balance; phone and desktop close overlook; desktop lake; phone and desktop walking platform; phone photography modal; phone and desktop return to the world. Live SF night was restored after day preview. Motion on, mute off-listening state and 42% master/100% subchannels were restored/preserved. No warnings/errors logged after 12:38Z during final interactions. Viewport testing is not physical touch/audio/haptic or older-M1 validation.

Static cost: 9,337 valley triangles, four valley materials; refined patch 25,088 triangles (34,425 combined); entire globe terrain 106,398 triangles and 2,370,452 attribute/index bytes. No new shadow lights or downloaded textures. Added rendered-surface tests validate finite geometry/indices, nonzero waterfall width, cliff clearance, entire wet-channel contact and a FrontSide lookout raycast against visitor feet. 118 tests and TypeScript passed before the final build.

Remaining visual gaps: large rock silhouettes/materials still read as procedural; the shallow stream outlet, foam and shore detail need more craft; paths are too uniform; distant night-world models remain dark and small. Keep these art gates open, along with physical-device testing and the wider atlas work. Do not characterize this checkpoint as AAA quality or completion of the full queue.
