# Sri’s world — experience direction

September 14, 2026. This brief records the latest direction from Sri and guides the rebuild. It is not a completion report.

## The visitor’s story

The first thing to understand is who Sri is now: a Founding Engineer at Offseason, building an AI agent for personal wellness in San Francisco. The world then gives that introduction depth. A visitor can follow a suggested route or go directly to a subject. Reading never depends on winning a game or finding a hidden object.

1. **Home / now.** A rainy, inhabited house with the three-monitor setup. Current work, approach to building, and an entrance into experience. Clickable screens and objects have a reason to exist.
2. **Project town.** Six individually identified workshop/gallery buildings around a square, plus an iPhone studio and hackathon hall. Each leads to its project’s problem, interaction, implementation, and source. The full searchable collection remains available in one action.
3. **The person.** About Sri and his path sit alongside the work, with research, ASU, entrepreneurship, and communities presented as chapters. Do not invent missing dates or outcomes.
4. **After hours.** A listening house and small cinema share a neighborhood. A turntable connects to Sri’s own music; the screen connects to the comfort-show rotation. Fred again.. and Tollywood are personal taste, not implied endorsements or integrated Spotify activity.
5. **Play / outdoors.** The badminton court, basketball, hiking, and California wish list add life around the professional core.
6. **What’s next.** A quiet, visibly unfinished plot for content. “Not open yet” describes a real plan, not a gamified lock on existing work. Leave geographic room for more places.

## Navigation and content

Remove World / Spatial / Desktop modes, the desktop imitation, and the icon launcher. Use a small header (personal signature, Work, About, Index), a chapter guide with direct selection and sequential onward travel, and a map button. The index offers all content without exploring. One reading sheet at a time preserves context and eliminates window management. Project deep links and the accessible `/story` remain.

The first view introduces the person and current role over the whole connected world. “Start at home” begins the journey; “See my work” is a direct route. Each destination gets a short, personal introduction, a relevant content action, and local objects. Avoid repeating Sri’s job title over every biome.

## Art direction

Use the supplied cabin references for composition and material standards: midnight blue atmosphere, slate shadows, glacial blue and lavender accents, restrained amber pools of light, walnut and textured fabric. Architecture has credible proportions, thin metal joins, glass with depth, soft upholstery, cables and small purposeful details. Character proportions may be cute; furniture should not look inflated. Plants need leaf silhouettes, not stacked ellipsoids.

The world stays stylized and connected. No photographic Earth, no separate indoor scene swaps, no floating display slabs. Roads and clearings belong to the same deformed surface. Roofs reveal interiors continuously as the camera approaches. A house is still legible from orbit. Detail is judged at the actual close camera, not only at thumbnail size.

Typography: Bricolage Grotesque for a distinctive, warm voice in large headings; Manrope for calm, readable UI and body text. Both self-hosted with OFL licenses. A compact custom lowercase signature replaces the crossed monogram.

## Motion and sound

Maintain continuous camera travel and user-controlled orbit. Small gestures settle naturally. Interactive objects respond to a specific action; decorative motion stays subtle. Reduced-motion and still modes are first-class.

Sound is off until explicitly enabled. One master environment control crossfades between rain at home, a soft town bed, outdoor air, and warm interior room tone. Navigation has a short, quiet cue; object interactions have distinct cues. Stop audio when the page is hidden, clean up nodes, and duck ambience while Sri’s track plays. No unrequested music playback and no sound on every hover.
